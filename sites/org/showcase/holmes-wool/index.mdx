When attaching the liner to the outer part of the hats I found it helpful to use plastic clips from a local quilt shop.  After clipping it together, I serged the liner to the outside part of the hat.
![quilting clips holding liner to outer](https://imagedelivery.net/ouSuR9yY1bHt-fuAokSA5Q/showcase-holmes-wool-1768175720890/public "Clips from the quilt shop holding liner to outer")

One deviation I made from the Holmes design is I added a band on the inside like what I've seen in some commercial hats.  To make the band I used some extra black fabric I had lying around, wrapped it around a strip of cotton batting, and covered the seam with bias tape.
![hat with sweatband partially stitched in place](https://imagedelivery.net/ouSuR9yY1bHt-fuAokSA5Q/showcase-holmes-wool-1768175852054/public "Partial progress in hand stitching the band in place.")

After finishing the hats, I added labels to the inside to help us tell them apart.
![Inside of finished hats showing labels](https://imagedelivery.net/ouSuR9yY1bHt-fuAokSA5Q/showcase-holmes-wool-1768176022484/public "I had to make sure we could tell our hats apart.")

I'm quite pleased how they turned out.  My first experience with FreeSewing was a success!
![Finished hat on danmc](https://imagedelivery.net/ouSuR9yY1bHt-fuAokSA5Q/showcase-holmes-wool-1768176086829/public "Ready to solve any mystery")
