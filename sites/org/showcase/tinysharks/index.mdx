There have been several discussions about making these plush toys in *unusual* sizes. Going big isn't that complicated, but going much smaller has its limitations. I had to modify the pattern, leaving out the teeth, combining the mouth and belly parts into one, making the body a bit wider, and changing the way the fins are attached.

The material is regular minky, all hand sewn, and the eyes are 5mm rhinestone rivets, attached with a faux leather backing piece (like a washer), so they don't work their way out of the stretchy fabric.
