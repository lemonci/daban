FreeSewing v4.4 has been released, and with it comes a new design: Sophie.

<!-- truncate -->

## Sophie Slip Dress

Sophie is _a FreeSewing pattern for a slip dress_ designed and coded by
JennaBarbara. Her [first ever open source
contribution](https://codeberg.org/freesewing/freesewing/pulls/606) :tada:.

Here's what she had to say about it:

> I like making and wearing slip dresses. They’re easy to put together, can be
> suitable for various levels of formality (from pyjamas to formalwear), and
> can be made from many different fabrics.
>
> I decided I wanted to try drafting a slip using the Freesewing developer
> tools instead of paper, and the Sophie design was the result.
