---
title: Waist circumference
---

The **waist circumference** measurement is the circumference of your waist below your ribcage.

To measure your **waist circumference**, wrap the tape measure around the narrowest part of your waist,
or wherever your skin folds when you bend sideways, making sure to keep it horizontal.

:::tip
The waist measurement is usually above the navel.

If the waist is not the smallest part of your body, our
[measurement faq entry](/docs/about/faq/measurements-issues/#waist) contains some tips
for locating your natural waist.
:::
