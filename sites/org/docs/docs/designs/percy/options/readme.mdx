## Conditional Options {#conditional}
### Front Pleat Preview Length {#frontpleatdisplaylength}

**How far to draw the pleat preview lines. This doesn't affect shaping at all.**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **8%**
- Minimum: **1%**
- Maximum: **20%**

<Frontpleatdisplaylength />

### Front Pleat Position {#frontpleatposition}

**Where to generate a pleat on the front panel.**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **50%**
- Minimum: **10%**
- Maximum: **90%**

<Frontpleatposition />

### Front Pleat Width {#frontpleatwidth}

**How big of a pleat to generate on the front panel.**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **6.4%**
- Minimum: **0%**
- Maximum: **10%**

<Frontpleatwidth />

### Hem Ratio {#hemratio}

**How far the bottom hem should be flared, relative to the same height on a basic Titan. This is very approximate. The flare won't go past 90 degrees total.**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **250%**
- Minimum: **100%**
- Maximum: **400%**

<Hemratio />

### Slash Iterations {#slashiterations}

**How many cuts to make to spread the body panels. Turn this up if it looks jagged.**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **undefined**
- Minimum: **1**
- Maximum: **24**

<Slashiterations />

## Fit {#fit}
### Knee ease {#kneeease}

**Controls the amount of ease at the knee**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **6%**
- Minimum: **1%**
- Maximum: **25%**

<Kneeease />

### Seat ease {#seatease}

**Controls the amount of ease at your seat**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **7%**
- Minimum: **2%**
- Maximum: **12%**

<Seatease />

### Waist ease {#waistease}

**Controls the amount of ease at your waist**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **2%**
- Minimum: **0%**
- Maximum: **10%**

<Waistease />

## Style {#style}
### Crotch drop {#crotchdrop}

**Lowers the crotch for a more relaxed fit**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **2%**
- Minimum: **0%**
- Maximum: **15%**

<Crotchdrop />

### Cuff Width {#cuffwidth}

**How wide to make the leg cuffs.**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **5%**
- Minimum: **1%**
- Maximum: **15%**

<Cuffwidth />

### Fit the knee {#fitknee}

**Fits the legs from based on the knee circumference, rather than seat circumference**
- Type: **Boolean**
- Default: **false**

<Fitknee />

### Length at Inseam {#lengthinseam}

**How long the inseam of the shorts should be. 100% = floor length, 40-50% = knee length.**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **25%**
- Minimum: **5%**
- Maximum: **100%**

<Lengthinseam />

### Pocket Depth {#pocketdepth}

**How far below the waist the pockets should end. Measure from your waist to where your fingertips fall on your side.**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **60%**
- Minimum: **20%**
- Maximum: **80%**

<Pocketdepth />

### Use waistband angle for {#usewaistanglefor}

**Apply the waistband angle to the front, back or both**
- Type: **List**
- Default: **both**
- options:
  - both
  - backOnly
  - frontOnly

<Usewaistanglefor />

### Waistband angle {#waistangle}

**Change the angle of the waistband when viewed from the side**
- Type: **Degrees**
- Default: **0°**
- Minimum: **-20°**
- Maximum: **20°**

<Waistangle />

### Waist height {#waistheight}

**Controls the height of the waist, 100% = waist height, 0% = hip height**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **75%**
- Minimum: **0%**
- Maximum: **100%**

<Waistheight />

### Panel {#panel}
#### Front Panel Width {#frontpanelpercentage}

**What proportion of the front of the pants should be taken up by the front panel.**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **50%**
- Minimum: **33%**
- Maximum: **75%**

<Frontpanelpercentage />

#### Pocket Opening Depth {#pocketopeningdepth}

**How deep the pocket openings should be. They can't be shallower than the necessary front opening.**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **11%**
- Minimum: **5%**
- Maximum: **25%**

<Pocketopeningdepth />

#### Waistband width {#waistbandwidth}

**The width of the waistband**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **10%**
- Minimum: **4%**
- Maximum: **20%**

<Waistbandwidth />

### Pleat {#pleat}
#### Front Pleat {#frontpleat}

**Whether to generate a pleat on the front panel.**
- Type: **Boolean**
- Default: **true**

<Frontpleat />

### Spread {#spread}
#### Spread {#spread}

**Whether to slash and spread the body panels.**
- Type: **Boolean**
- Default: **true**

<Spread />

## Advanced {#advanced}
### Cross seam angle {#crossseamcurveangle}

**Controls the angle of the cross seam**
- Type: **Degrees**
- Default: **12°**
- Minimum: **0°**
- Maximum: **20°**

<Crossseamcurveangle />

### Cross seam bend {#crossseamcurvebend}

**Controls the curvature of the cross seam**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **65%**
- Minimum: **45%**
- Maximum: **85%**

<Crossseamcurvebend />

### Start of the cross seam curve {#crossseamcurvestart}

**Controls how far into the cross seam we start to curve**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **85%**
- Minimum: **60%**
- Maximum: **100%**

<Crossseamcurvestart />

### Crotch seam angle {#crotchseamcurveangle}

**Controls the angle of the crotch seam**
- Type: **Degrees**
- Default: **25°**
- Minimum: **0°**
- Maximum: **35°**

<Crotchseamcurveangle />

### Crotch seam bend {#crotchseamcurvebend}

**Controls the curvature of the crotch seam**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **80%**
- Minimum: **45%**
- Maximum: **100%**

<Crotchseamcurvebend />

### Start of the crotch seam curve {#crotchseamcurvestart}

**Controls how far into the crotch seam we start to curve**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **80%**
- Minimum: **60%**
- Maximum: **95%**

<Crotchseamcurvestart />

### Grainline position {#grainlineposition}

**Controls the horizontal position of the leg relative to the seat**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **45%**
- Minimum: **30%**
- Maximum: **60%**

<Grainlineposition />

### Leg balance {#legbalance}

**Controls the ratio between front and back panel of the leg**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **57.5%**
- Minimum: **52.5%**
- Maximum: **62.5%**

<Legbalance />

### Waist balance {#waistbalance}

**Controls the horizontal position of the waist relative to the seat**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **60%**
- Minimum: **30%**
- Maximum: **90%**

<Waistbalance />
