---
title: 'Leglength'
sidebar_class_name: hidden
---

The length of the pant legs can be changed with this option. You
can choose one of the pre-defined settings, or enter a custom value.

- 'Long' is a full-length pair of pants.
- 'Capris' end just below the knee.
- 'Shorts' end just above the knee.
- 'Custom' allows you to change the length to your own desire.
