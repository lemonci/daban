## What You Need {#needs}

To make the Sophie slip, you will need the following:

- [Basic sewing supplies](/docs/sewing/basic-sewing-supplies)
- A strap turner, a choptick or something similar to turn the straps inside out
- A few meters of a suitable fabric (see [Fabric Options](#fabric))
- (Optional) About 0.5 - 1 metre (0.6 - 1.1 yards) of lining fabric if you choose to line the cups and straps with a different fabric from your main fabric

## Fabric Options {#fabric}

### Main Fabric

A light woven fabric will work for this pattern

- **Main Fabric**
  - Cut **2 Cup**
  - Cut **2 Back Strap**
  - Cut **1 Front Panel** on a fold, on the bias
  - Cut **1 Back Panel** on a fold, on the bias

### Lining Fabric

A light woven fabric, it can be the same as your main fabric

- **Lining Fabric**
  - Cut **2 Cup**
  - Cut **2 Back Strap**
