## Fit {#fit}
### Armhole Drop {#armholedrop}

**How much to lower the armhole**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **80%**
- Minimum: **50%**
- Maximum: **90%**

<Armholedrop />

### Armhole Width Back {#armholewidthback}

**How far the armhole will extend toward the center of the back**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **30%**
- Minimum: **20%**
- Maximum: **90%**

<Armholewidthback />

### Armhole Width Front {#armholewidthfront}

**How far the armhole will extend toward the center of the chest**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **30%**
- Minimum: **20%**
- Maximum: **90%**

<Armholewidthfront />

## Style {#style}
### Back Coverage {#backcoverage}

**Controls how far up the back will be covered**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **10%**
- Minimum: **-50%**
- Maximum: **90%**

<Backcoverage />

### Bottom Width Bonus {#bottomwidthbonus}

**How much room will there be around the legs at the bottom of the dress**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **20%**
- Minimum: **0%**
- Maximum: **100%**

<Bottomwidthbonus />

### Length Bonus {#lengthbonus}

**This controls the length of the dress as a percentage of the natural waist to knee measurement**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **0%**
- Minimum: **-50%**
- Maximum: **50%**

<Lengthbonus />

### Neckline Coverage {#necklinecoverage}

**How far up the neckline will be covered**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **20%**
- Minimum: **0%**
- Maximum: **90%**

<Necklinecoverage />

### Side Corner Drop {#sidecornerdrop}

**How far down the cups go on the side between the underbust and the waist**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **50%**
- Minimum: **0%**
- Maximum: **100%**

<Sidecornerdrop />

### Strap Width {#strapwidth}

**How wide do you want your shoulder straps?**
- Type: **Percentage**
- Default: **30%**
- Minimum: **10%**
- Maximum: **100%**

<Strapwidth />
