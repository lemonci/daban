## What You Need {#needs}

To make Ashley, you will need the following:

- [Basic sewing supplies](/docs/sewing/basic-sewing-supplies)
- About 1.5 meters of a suitable fabric ([see Fabric options](#fabric))

## Fabric Options {#fabric}

Ashley is designed with medium or heavyweight woven fabric in mind. Good options include (but are not limited to) cotton twill, cotton canvas, or corduroy.

## Cutting Instructions {#cutting}

- Cut **2 backs**
- Cut **2 fronts**
- Cut **2 yokes**
- Cut **2 pockets**
- Cut **2 pocket facings**
- Cut **2 back pockets**
- Cut **1 waistband right**
- Cut **1 waistband left**
- Cut **2 waistband backs**
- Cut **1 fly extension** on the fold
- Cut **1 fly facing**
- Cut **1 belt loops** (That will be cut up into 7 pieces)

:::warning

#### Caveats

- There is extra hem allowance at the hem
  :::
